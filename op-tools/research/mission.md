# Mission: Research Tool

Build a research engine that runs after Strategy and before planning, producing milestone-aligned research packs that improve implementation quality and reduce planning blind spots.

Research is responsible for transforming strategic intent into evidence-backed implementation guidance. It should decompose mission and strategy milestones into concrete research topics, gather public references and technical patterns, and summarize practical options with tradeoffs.

If the mission implies interface design, architecture choices, security controls, domain regulations, performance constraints, or media direction, Research must produce targeted topic packs for each domain and connect them back to milestone IDs.

Research outputs may include:
- implementation pattern summaries
- architecture and infrastructure comparisons
- UX and visual references
- relevant public image/audio/video references with provenance
- risk notes and fallback options

The mission succeeds when planner quality improves measurably: fewer ambiguous tasks, clearer implementation pathways, fewer avoidable blockers, and better alignment to mission intent.
